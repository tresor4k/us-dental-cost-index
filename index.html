US Dental Cost Index by State (2026)

How much does dental care cost across the United States, and why? This open dataset reports the average single-implant price for all 50 states + DC in 2026, with a Restorative Cost Index (100 = national average).

Correction notice (July 2026): earlier versions of this explorer showed per-state veneer and braces prices and a composite index built on them. Those series were derived from the implant series, not observed, and were retracted - see the public corrections log. Only observed data is shown below.
Key finding: a state's dental Restorative Cost Index tracks its cost of living at Pearson r = 0.835 — dental fees follow local rent and wages far more than anything clinical. A single implant averages $3,759 in Alabama vs $5,733 in California.

About this dataset

The Restorative Cost Index = the state's average single-implant price ÷ the national average × 100 (so 100 = the U.S. average). Deliberately simple and fully reproducible from the open CSV in this repository.
